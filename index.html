Reversing Diseases, Depression & Damaged Relationships

A life-changing seminar on wholistic healing designed to restore health, bring mental and emotional renewal, and rebuild broken relationships through practical lifestyle principles and the healing ministry of Jesus.

About This Program / Kuhusu Semina Hii

English: We live in a world filled with disease, stress, broken families, addictions, and emotional pain. Many people feel trapped in chronic illness, depression, or damaged relationships.

English: During this training you will learn how to:

  • Reverse chronic diseases naturally / Kushinda magonjwa sugu kwa njia ya asili

  • Build stronger families / Kujenga familia imara

Accommodation / Malazi

English: Thank you very much for your application to attend the “Reversing Diseases, Depression & Damaged Relationships” Seminar. The seminar will take place from May 1 to May 3, 2026.

The seminar fee is 75,000 TSh for adults, and this includes accommodation and vegan meals during the seminar. Children below 6 years attend for free, and children aged 6 years and above pay 30,000 TSh.

Camping space will be provided free of charge, and there are toilets and showers available. Please remember to bring your own tent.

All meals during the seminar will be vegan meals. If you have any dietary restrictions, please inform us in advance so we can prepare accordingly.

There will also be children’s programs during the seminar.

Translation from English will be provided during the presentations.

For those who are interested, Dr. Mark Sandoval will bring books related to the seminar topics, and they will be available during the event.
