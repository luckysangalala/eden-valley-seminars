Reversing Diseases, Depression & Damaged Relationships

A life-changing seminar on wholistic healing designed to restore health, bring mental and emotional renewal, and rebuild broken relationships through practical lifestyle principles and the healing ministry of Jesus.

About This Program / Kuhusu Semina Hii

English: We live in a world filled with disease, stress, broken families, addictions, and emotional pain. Many people feel trapped in chronic illness, depression, or damaged relationships.

Swahili: Tunaishi katika dunia iliyojaa magonjwa, msongo wa mawazo, familia zilizovunjika, uraibu, na maumivu ya hisia. Watu wengi wanahisi wamekwama katika magonjwa sugu, unyogovu, au uhusiano ulioharibiwa.

English: During this training you will learn how to:

Swahili: Katika semina hii utajifunza jinsi ya:

  • Reverse chronic diseases naturally / Kushinda magonjwa sugu kwa njia ya asili
  • Overcome depression and mental fatigue / Kushinda unyogovu na uchovu wa akili
  • Restore damaged relationships / Kurudisha uhusiano ulioharibiwa
  • Understand lifestyle medicine / Kuelewa tiba ya mtindo wa maisha
  • Use natural remedies for healing / Kutumia tiba za asili kwa uponyaji
  • Build stronger families / Kujenga familia imara
The relation that exists between the mind and the body is very intimate. When one is affected, the other sympathizes. The condition of the mind affects the health to a far greater degree than many realize.
